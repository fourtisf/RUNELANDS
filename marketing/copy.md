## Launch thread (reply under the launch tweet — attach the matching banner)

1/ 🪓 BUILD YOUR REALM KEEP
Chop trees, farm the land, and sell wood to the Merchant for coins. Fight slimes for XP and level up — it's all server-authoritative, so no client-side cheating on what matters. *(image: `thread-1-build.png`)*

2/ 📜 CLAIM LAND THAT PAYS RENT
Spend a Land Deed to claim a plot. It's protected — nobody else can build on it — and it pays you passive rent every minute. Own more → earn more. *(image: `thread-2-land.png`)*

3/ ⚔️ HUNT THE WARLORD
Every few minutes a Warlord boss roams the realm. Huge HP, huge loot. Rally other players and take it down together. *(image: `thread-3-warlord.png`)*

4/ 🪙 YOUR LAND, ON-CHAIN
RUNELANDS is an own-land economy. On-chain land (Solana/USDC) is on the roadmap — your in-game plots become the real thing. Play free now, no wallet needed 👉 runelands.fun *(image: `thread-4-economy.png`)*
